About

نبذة

I'm Omar, an engineer from Giza who can't leave a thing alone until I understand how it works, and then I want to build it properly.

My path hasn't been a straight line. I started with the fundamentals: the math behind machine learning, then deep learning, then reinforcement learning, with side trips into running models in the browser and classics like operating systems and computer graphics. My graduation project took me into generative vision, where a teammate and I taught Stable Diffusion to draw floor plans, and that work became a published paper. Today I spend most of my time on LLM agents. The thread through all of it is the same: learn by building, and don't stop at "it runs."

I also teach. As a teaching assistant I've worked with more than 100 students, and explaining something to a room full of people is the fastest way I know to find out whether I really understand it. It has made me a better engineer, and a more honest one. I'd rather say "I'm still learning this" than claim something I can't back up.

Outside of work I read, listen to podcasts, and play video games. I also live in three languages: Arabic at home, English at work, and German whenever I get the chance.

Handbook Assistant

A customer support agent that knows when to ask a human

Answers student questions from the CS and IS handbooks, and opens a ticket for a human advisor when it can't answer confidently.

retrieve→gate: context→generate→gate: faithful ∥ relevant→answer↳ else escalate
  • Three independent LLM judges decide when to escalate, and they fail closed if they break.
  • Tickets survive restarts, and the advisor's reply goes back into the student's conversation by thread_id.
  • Advisors choose which answers join the knowledge base, and handbook text always ranks first.

Mizan ميزان

An Egyptian labor law assistant

Answers questions about Egyptian labor law in Arabic and English, grounded in the text of the law. Mīzān means "balance" in Arabic.

load profile→retrieve→grade→generate→save profile↺ rewrite if weak
  • A corrective RAG loop grades each retrieved passage and rewrites the question when results are weak.
  • Remembers each user across sessions with Trustcall.
  • Deployed as a Gradio app on Hugging Face Spaces.

Research

البحث العلمي

Machine Graphics & Vision · Vol. 34, No. 3 · 2025 · peer-reviewed

Fine-tuning Stable Diffusion for Generating 2D Floor Plans Using Prompt Templates

A. Mostafa, O. Amir, A. M. Mohamed, M. O. Al Enany

We fine-tuned Stable Diffusion 1.5 on a custom floor plan dataset, using structured prompt templates to steer the model toward concise outputs that tolerate small errors. The paper also proposes combining diffusion models with graph neural networks, so that generated layouts respect how rooms connect to each other.
